## Gemini

Open [Gemini](https://gemini.google.com) → ask it to **create an image** (or use image generation). Copy a **Gemini prompt** below, paste, download the result into this folder.

Use **16:9 or 3:2**, photorealistic, no text/watermarks/logos, National Geographic travel style.

## Shots

1. **Ao Nang Beach**
   - **Gemini:** Photorealistic editorial travel photo, Ao Nang Beach Krabi Thailand at golden hour, soft warm light, limestone karsts in the distance, calm Andaman Sea, a few long-tail boats on the sand, empty peaceful mood, shot on 35mm, high detail, no people, no text.
   - **Google:** `Ao Nang beach Krabi Thailand sunset`

2. **Limestone cliffs**
   - **Gemini:** Dramatic photorealistic wide shot of towering limestone karsts rising from turquoise Andaman water near Krabi Thailand, jungle on cliff tops, long-tail boat small in frame, crisp daylight, travel magazine cover quality, no text.
   - **Google:** `Krabi limestone cliffs Andaman Sea Thailand`

3. **Long-tail boats**
   - **Gemini:** Photorealistic photo from Ao Nang pier Krabi, colorful Thai long-tail boats lined up on beach, shallow clear water, cliffs behind, morning light, documentary travel style, no logos.
   - **Google:** `longtail boat Ao Nang Krabi pier`

4. **Beach seafood**
   - **Gemini:** Photorealistic close-medium shot of fresh grilled seafood platter on beach restaurant table Ao Nang Krabi, prawns and fish, lime and chili, ocean bokeh background, warm evening light, food travel editorial, no text on image.
   - **Google:** `Thai grilled seafood beach restaurant Krabi`

5. **Night market**
   - **Gemini:** Photorealistic night scene Ao Nang street market Krabi, glowing food stalls, skewers and smoke, warm tungsten lights, crowded but authentic Thailand atmosphere, shallow depth of field, no readable signs.
   - **Google:** `Ao Nang night market street food`

6. **Resort & pool view**
   - **Gemini:** Photorealistic luxury pool overlooking Andaman Sea Krabi, infinity edge, limestone cliffs on horizon, palm trees, late afternoon sun, boutique hotel vibe, serene, travel magazine, no brand names.
   - **Google:** `Krabi hotel pool Andaman sea view`
